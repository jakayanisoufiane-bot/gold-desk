Rules of the desk

A trade you can't justify in one sentence is a lottery ticket. All three columns pass or you don't press the button.

Account discipline

  1. Risk 1.0% of capital per idea, not per ticket — $5.00 right now. Two entries on the same idea share it.
  2. Recalculate that amount on the 1st of the month from real capital. Never raise it mid-drawdown.
  3. Two losses in a day and you're done — $10.00, the daily stop.
  4. Max 3 trades a day. After the second, only an A setup earns a third.
  5. Every position has its stop set in the platform before it's live. No stop, no trade.
  6. Withdraw only at milestones set in advance — never mid-drawdown.

The % follows the risk slider on the Calculator page, the $ follows your capital.

Pre-flight check

  1. Setup matches one of the seven in the book.
  2. Session lines up with the setup.
  3. R:R on this ticket is at least 1.5.
  4. No high-impact news within 15 minutes.
  5. State of mind is Neutral or Confident.

After the trade

  1. Log it here the moment it closes.
  2. Screenshot the chart in MT5.
  3. Write one sentence: what worked, what didn't.
  4. Review every Friday: winners share a pattern.
  5. Cut size, never raise it, if drawdown passes 15%.
